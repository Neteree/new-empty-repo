---
title: Keeping bread fresh without plastic
date: 2026-08-28
excerpt: Plastic bags make crusts go soft. A tea towel, a bread box, or your freezer all do a better job.
art: baguette
---

A plastic bag traps moisture, which turns a crackly crust soft and chewy within hours.

**Day one and two:** keep sourdough cut side down on a board, loosely wrapped in a tea towel.

**Day three onwards:** slice what's left and freeze it. Toast slices straight from the freezer, with no need to thaw them first.

**Baguettes** are best on the day. If you have some left over, revive it in a hot oven for five minutes, or make tomorrow's breadcrumbs.
