title: Dahlia season is short, so here is how to make it last

excerpt: Dahlias don't keep opening after they're cut, so choose them nearly fully open and change the water every day.

Unlike peonies, dahlias won't open further once they're cut. What you buy is what you get, so choose stems that are already about three-quarters open.

**Fresh water daily.** Dahlia stems foul water quickly. Rinse the vase and refill it every morning.

**Hot water trick.** Some growers dip freshly cut stems in hot water, around 80°C, for a few seconds. It helps the stems keep drinking.

Expect four to six days in the vase. That's short, but few flowers look this good while they last.
