Peonies arrive with us tight, at what growers call the marshmallow stage: the bud gives slightly when you press it, like a marshmallow. Buy them there and you get the whole show at home.

**Recut every stem.** Take 2cm off on a sharp angle and strip any leaves that would sit below the waterline.

**Start cool, then warm.** Cool water and a cool room slow them down. If you want blooms for Saturday, move the vase somewhere warmer on Thursday.

**Ants are normal.** Peonies from the field sometimes carry a few ants. A gentle swish, head down, in a sink of water sends them on their way.
