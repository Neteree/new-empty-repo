---
title: Why our arrangements are foam-free
date: 2026-10-02
excerpt: Floral foam breaks down into microplastics. We use chicken wire, flower frogs and good stem structure instead.

Green floral foam made arranging easy for decades, but it's a plastic that crumbles into microplastic dust and can't be composted.

We arrange with reusable mechanics instead: a loose ball of chicken wire, a metal flower frog (a spiked holder for the vase base), or a grid of tape across the vase mouth. Stems sit in real water, so they drink better and last longer.

Every one of our vessels can be washed and reused, and you're welcome to bring them back to us.
